# Working Group Resource Collections

Training offerings, open educational resources and materials already exist, but are often only known or accessible to a small circle of people (e.g. Train-the-Trainer concepts for RDM or Carpentries materials). This working group identifies such offerings within the NFDI consortia, RDM projects, data competence centers, RDM state initiatives and others, discusses them and share good practices.
