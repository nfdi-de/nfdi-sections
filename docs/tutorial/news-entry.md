title: Create and Edit a News Entry
sidebar_position: 4
---

Creating and editing [News](https://nfdi-de.github.io/nfdi-sections/blog) works just like [./create-page.md] and [./edit-page.md], but you have to edit files in the folder /blog and the header info should be e.g.
```
---
slug: section-meta-meeting-march-2026
title: "Invitation: Section Meta Meeting – 31 March 2026"
authors: [okoepler]
tags: [Meta]
---
```
